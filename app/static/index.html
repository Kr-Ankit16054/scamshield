DEFENSE GUIDE

Practical SMS Scam Defense Tips

Key principles to protect yourself from modern smishing and financial fraud campaigns.

Never Share OTPs or PINs

Legitimate banks, delivery services, and government departments will never ask you to disclose verification codes, passwords, or netbanking credentials over phone or SMS.

Beware Personal Mobile Senders

Official notices from banks, electricity boards, and telecom authorities originate from registered alphanumeric sender IDs (e.g. VK-HDFCBK), never from personal 10-digit mobile numbers.

QR Codes Only Pay Money

Scanning a QR code or entering your UPI PIN always deducts funds from your bank account. You never need to scan a QR code or enter a PIN to receive a payment or refund.

Verify via Official Portals

If an SMS claims your electricity, SIM, or bank account will be blocked, navigate independently to the official app or portal. Never call numbers or click links provided inside the text.
